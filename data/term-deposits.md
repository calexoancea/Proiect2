Term deposits can be opened in RON, EUR or USD, with maturities from one month to two
years. The minimum opening amount is 500 lei (or the equivalent in EUR/USD).

Breaking a deposit before maturity forfeits the accrued interest; the principal is
returned in full, but no interest is paid for the elapsed period.

Interest rates for RON term deposits (as of the 2026 schedule):
- 1 month: 4.2% per year
- 6 months: 4.8% per year
- 12 months: 5.1% per year
- 24 months: 5.3% per year
