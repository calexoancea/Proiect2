Monthly maintenance fees by account type:

| Account type      | Monthly fee | Free transactions/month | Card issuance fee |
|-------------------|-------------|--------------------------|--------------------|
| Basic current      | 5 lei       | 10                       | 20 lei             |
| Standard current    | 10 lei      | 25                       | free               |
| Premium current     | 25 lei      | unlimited                | free               |
| Student current     | 0 lei       | unlimited                | free               |
| Savings account      | 0 lei       | 3 withdrawals            | not applicable     |

Students must present a valid enrollment certificate renewed each academic year to keep
the Student current account free of charge; otherwise it converts automatically to
Basic current terms.
