If a card is lost, stolen, or damaged, follow this procedure end to end:

1. The customer reports the incident via the app, call centre, or in a branch. Record
   the exact time of the report — this determines liability for any transaction made
   before the report.
2. Immediately block the card in the core banking system, regardless of the channel used
   to report it.
3. Verify the customer's identity with two factors before proceeding.
4. Ask whether the customer wants a replacement card with the same number (only possible
   for damaged cards, not lost or stolen) or a new card number.
5. For lost or stolen cards, always issue a new card number; never reuse the old one.
6. Standard replacement cards arrive within 5 business days by post, free of charge for
   Standard and Premium accounts; Basic accounts pay a 20 lei reissue fee.
7. Express replacement (next-day, branch pickup) costs 50 lei regardless of account
   type.
8. Once the new card is activated, the old card is permanently deactivated and cannot be
   reused even if found later.

The customer remains liable for transactions made before the report time, subject to
standard fraud investigation rules described in the complaints procedure.
