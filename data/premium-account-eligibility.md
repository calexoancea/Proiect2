---
title: Premium account eligibility
product: accounts
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 1
---

The Premium current account is available to customers who meet at least one of these
criteria:
- Average monthly income of at least 8,000 lei over the last 3 months, or
- Total balance across accounts and deposits of at least 100,000 lei, or
- An active mortgage or business loan with Libra Bank exceeding 200,000 lei.

Premium status is reviewed every 12 months; customers who no longer qualify are moved
to the Standard current account with 60 days' written notice.
