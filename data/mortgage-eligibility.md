To qualify for a Libra Bank mortgage, an applicant must meet all of the following:
- Minimum age 21, maximum age at loan maturity 65.
- Stable income for at least 12 months, documented by payslips or a tax return for the
  self-employed.
- Debt-to-income ratio below 40% including the new mortgage installment.
- Romanian citizenship or a valid residence permit of at least 3 years remaining.

The down payment percentage and origination fee are defined in the current mortgage fee
schedule (see the 2026 fee schedule, which supersedes the 2025 one).
