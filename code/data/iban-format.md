---
title: IBAN format for Libra Bank accounts
product: general
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 1
---

All Libra Bank IBANs follow the standard Romanian format: two letters (RO), two check
digits, four letters identifying the bank (LIBR), and sixteen alphanumeric characters
identifying the branch and account.

Example: RO49LIBR0000123456789012

Customers can find their IBAN in the mobile app under Account > Details, or on the
first page of their account statement. Bank staff should never read out a full IBAN over
an unverified phone call without completing identity verification first.
