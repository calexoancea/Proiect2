---
title: Card blocking and unblocking procedure
product: cards
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-15
version: 3
---

A card is blocked automatically in three situations: three consecutive failed PIN
attempts, a suspicious transaction flagged by the fraud engine, or the customer tapping
"Block card" in the mobile app.

To unblock a card, follow these steps in order:
1. Confirm the customer's identity using two factors (ID number and date of birth, or
   the phone-banking password).
2. Check the block reason in the fraud dashboard or the app's recent alerts.
3. If the block was due to failed PIN attempts, reset the PIN via the branch terminal or
   guide the customer through the mobile app's "Reset PIN" flow.
4. If the block was due to a fraud alert, do not unblock until the fraud team confirms
   the transaction was legitimate.
5. If the customer blocked it themselves, simply toggle "Unblock card" in the app, or
   have the call centre agent do it from the CRM.
6. Log the resolution in the case management system with a reference number.

The customer can also visit any branch and complete identity verification in person, or
call the dedicated card line and authenticate using the phone-banking password.
