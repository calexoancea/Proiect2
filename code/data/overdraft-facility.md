---
title: Personal overdraft facility
product: accounts
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 1
---

An overdraft facility is available on Standard and Premium current accounts, subject to
credit approval. The maximum overdraft is typically one month's average net income,
capped at 15,000 lei.

Interest is charged only on the amount used, at 1.5% per month, calculated daily. There
is no fee for arranging the facility, but an annual review fee of 50 lei applies on the
renewal date.

Overdraft is not available on Basic or Student current accounts.
