product: general
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01

---

Customers can file a complaint through the app, by calling the complaints line, or in
writing at any branch. Every complaint must be logged with a unique reference number
within 24 hours of receipt.

Response time targets:
- Simple complaints (e.g. incorrect fee charged): resolved within 5 business days.
- Complex complaints (e.g. disputed transaction, fraud claim): resolved within 15
  business days, extendable to 30 with written notice to the customer.

If a customer disagrees with the resolution, they may escalate to the National Bank of
Romania's dispute resolution service (SAL-Fin), free of charge.
