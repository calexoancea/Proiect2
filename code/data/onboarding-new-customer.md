Opening a current account requires: a valid ID document, proof of address no older than
3 months, and a minimum initial deposit of 50 lei.

Onboarding can be completed entirely online via video identification, or in person at
any branch. Online onboarding takes an average of 15 minutes; branch onboarding
typically takes 30 minutes including card issuance.

New customers automatically receive a Standard current account unless they qualify for
and request the Student or Premium tiers, which have separate eligibility criteria
described in the account fee table.
