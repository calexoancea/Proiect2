---
title: Mortgage fee schedule
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 2
---

Mortgage loans require a down payment of at least twenty percent for a first home,
raised from fifteen percent in the previous schedule.

Early repayment is free of charge during the variable-rate period. During the
fixed-rate period, an early repayment fee of one percent of the remaining balance
applies (unchanged from 2025).

Origination fee: 0.4% of the loan amount, capped at 2,500 lei (reduced from 0.5%).

Late payment penalty: 0.1% of the overdue amount per day, starting from day 6
(unchanged).
