To avoid confusion, this document lists banking products that Libra Bank does NOT
offer as of 2026:

- Student loans (education financing) — not part of the current product catalogue.
- Cryptocurrency custody or trading services.
- Foreign currency mortgages for properties located outside Romania.
- Business overdraft facilities for companies with fewer than 6 months of activity.

Staff and the AI assistant must state clearly that these products are unavailable
rather than improvising an answer, and may redirect the customer to a partner
institution only if a formal partnership exists (none currently does).
