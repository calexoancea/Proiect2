---
title: How to calculate the early repayment cost
product: mortgages
audience: retail
effective: 2026-01-01
version: 1
---

To estimate the cost of repaying a mortgage early, combine two pieces of information:
1. Whether the loan is currently in its variable-rate or fixed-rate period (check the
   loan contract's rate schedule).
2. The remaining balance at the time of repayment.

If the loan is in the variable-rate period, there is no fee — the customer pays only the
remaining balance.

If the loan is in the fixed-rate period, multiply the remaining balance by 1% (per the
current mortgage fee schedule) to get the early repayment fee. Add this fee to the
remaining balance for the total amount due.

Example: a remaining balance of 50,000 lei repaid during the fixed-rate period costs
50,000 + (50,000 x 1%) = 50,500 lei. The same repayment during the variable-rate period
costs exactly 50,000 lei.
